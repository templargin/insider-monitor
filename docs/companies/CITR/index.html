CITR · CitroTech Inc.

CitroTech Inc. manufactures and sells fire inhibitor products in the United States and Canada. The company also offers fire defense for roadsides, corridors, and critical assets, lumber treatment and spraying solutions, as well as develops wildfire defense systems on residential and commercial properties under the CitroSafe Systems brand name. It serves wildland fire, residential home protection, wood products, ground application, lumber and building materials, and construction industries; homeowners; governments; and agencies. The company was formerly known as General Enterprise Ventures, Inc. and changed its name to CitroTech Inc. in January 2026. The company is based in Greenwood Village, Colorado.
