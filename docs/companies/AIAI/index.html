AIAI · AIAI Holdings Corp

AIAI Holdings Corporation engages in the creation of an AI-powered ecosystem by acquiring, integrating, and scaling companies internationally. Its proprietary AI application enhances the operating performance of portfolio companies across various verticals, such as construction, healthcare, defense, blockchain data infrastructure, digital assets, and government services. The company was formerly known as MXLII Corporation and changed its name to AIAI Holdings Corporation in November 2025. AIAI Holdings Corporation was incorporated in 2024 and is based in Dallas, Texas.
