RMIX · Suncrete, Inc.

Suncrete, Inc. provides ready-mix concrete logistics and distribution platform serving construction markets in the United States Sunbelt region. The company operates batching plants, tech-enabled mixer truck dispatch, and logistics. The company owns 595 mixer trucks. It serves customers of public infrastructure, commercial, and residential sectors. The company is based in Tulsa, Oklahoma.
